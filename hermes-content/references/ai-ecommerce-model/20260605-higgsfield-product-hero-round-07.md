## Objetivo de la ronda

Corregir el punto debil de Round 06: producto demasiado generico y poco fuerte para ecommerce/performance.

La direccion creativa de Round 07 prioriza:

- producto grande y en foco;
- botella simetrica;
- etiqueta limpia sin texto falso;
- manos naturales;
- rostro de la modelo como apoyo, no como sujeto principal;
- maxima calidad disponible en Higgsfield para imagen.

## Prompt usado

Create a high-end vertical ecommerce skincare ad using the reference image only as the identity anchor for the same fictional adult red-haired woman. PRIORITY: the skincare product must look excellent, sharp, symmetric, premium and commercially usable. Composition: close-up three-quarter portrait, the woman is slightly behind the product, smiling softly, very fair skin, natural freckles, wavy copper-red hair, modest cream knit top. The serum bottle is the hero in the foreground: clear glass cylindrical bottle, straight vertical edges, centered, crisp white dropper cap, clean blank cream label with NO readable text, no logo, no fake typography, no warped label, no melted glass, no distorted liquid. She holds the bottle with elegant natural fingers, product large and in focus, face still clear but secondary. Bright premium bathroom vanity, soft daylight, white marble, clean cream background, realistic skin texture, polished UGC luxury skincare aesthetic, ecommerce-ready, no text overlay, no watermark, non-sexual, brand-safe. Avoid: ugly product, distorted bottle, tiny product, blurry packaging, fake letters, extra fingers, deformed hands, asymmetrical face, oversexualized clothing, cleavage focus, logos, claims.

## Resultado visual

- Producto: mucho mas fuerte que Round 06. Es protagonista real de la imagen.
- Packaging: limpio, premium y sin texto falso. Util para mockup o ecommerce.
- Identidad: mantiene pelirroja, piel clara, pecas y look premium natural.
- Manos: aceptables y mas controladas que una generacion promedio.
- Realismo comercial: alto, especialmente para skincare y serum premium.
- Riesgo: el producto queda un poco grande y cercano; funciona como packshot hero, menos como UGC casual.
