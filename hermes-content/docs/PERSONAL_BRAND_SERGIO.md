# Personal Brand: Sergio

Sergio's personal brand is separate from Onia.

Onia is the system and the commercial proof. Sergio is the face, operator,
builder, and narrator.

## Content Role

Sergio should build trust by showing:

- how Onia is being built
- what decisions are being made
- what problems appear in real businesses
- what automation actually looks like behind the scenes
- what he is learning as a founder/operator

## Recommended Mix

- 70% informal videos.
- 20% stories and lifestyle/photo proof.
- 10% carousels with lessons or frameworks.

## What To Post

Good Sergio posts:

- "Estoy construyendo esto en Onia..."
- "Hoy aprendi esto automatizando Instagram..."
- "Este error casi rompe el flujo..."
- "Asi pienso una automatizacion antes de programarla..."
- "Lo que un negocio pierde cuando no responde WhatsApp rapido..."
- "Behind the scenes: dashboard, Telegram, Graph API, Notion, n8n."

Avoid:

- random selfies with no context
- fake luxury
- overproduced guru content
- claims without proof
- videos that feel like an ad every time

## Stories

The current `story-references.json` should be treated as personal-brand story
references unless the user says otherwise.

Use them for:

- routine
- gym/cafe/workspace
- laptop/dashboard
- daily progress
- quiet aesthetic proof
- polls and questions

## Carruseles

Sergio can post carousels, but less frequently than Onia.

Use personal carousels for:

- lessons from building Onia
- founder reflections
- simple frameworks
- "how I think about..." posts
- case-study breakdowns from Sergio's point of view

## Videos

Sergio videos should be real first.

Preferred structure:

1. Hook in first 2 seconds.
2. One clear idea.
3. Specific example from Onia or a client.
4. Practical lesson.
5. Soft CTA.

Example CTA:

- "Si quieres que te muestre el flujo, escribeme 'sistema'."
- "Voy a documentar como lo construimos."
- "Esto luego lo vamos a convertir en una automatizacion real."

## AI Face Rule

Do not publish AI-generated videos with Sergio's face without explicit approval.

AI can help:

- script
- edit
- subtitle
- create B-roll
- organize clips
- generate alternate hooks

AI should not silently impersonate Sergio.
