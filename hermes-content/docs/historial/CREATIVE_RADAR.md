# Onia Creative Radar

## Purpose

Hermes must act as a creative radar before important content production. The
goal is to find what is working now, understand why it works, adapt it without
copying, and turn it into production-ready Onia content.

## When to activate

Activate before:

- UGC ads;
- Higgsfield prompts;
- creator reference analysis;
- Reels, TikTok or Shorts strategy;
- campaign planning;
- AI video experiments;
- content workflows;
- creator/influencer inspiration;
- trend-dependent recommendations.

## Research stack

Priority sources:

1. Official tool/platform sources: Higgsfield, YouTube Blog, Instagram Creators,
   TikTok Creative Center, Meta business resources.
2. Real references Sergio shares.
3. Ad libraries and public creator examples.
4. Recent creator/trend reports.
5. Community observations only as weak signals, never as final proof.

## Scoring

Every skill, workflow, creator pattern or prompt system should be scored:

- utility for Onia content: 1-10;
- originality: 1-10;
- ease of application: 1-10;
- viral potential: 1-10;
- visual level: 1-10;
- conversion potential: 1-10;
- risk of looking copied: 1-10;
- priority: high, medium or low.

## Production rule

Research is not the output. The output must become:

- hooks;
- scripts;
- shot lists;
- Higgsfield prompts;
- negative prompts;
- workflow;
- variants;
- next production action.

## Creator reference rule

Use creator videos as pattern studies. Do not copy identity, voice, exact
phrases, gestures, jokes, music, brand, edit sequence or claims. Adapt only the
principles.
