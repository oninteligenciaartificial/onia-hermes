# Plans and Pricing

Pro, Scale-Up, Prime and Enterprise compared: monthly price, credits, users, workspaces and support, plus the 7-day trial and the yearly discount.

Dapta has three self-service plans and an Enterprise tier. Every plan includes unlimited agents and flows; what changes is the monthly credit allowance, the number of users and workspaces, storage and the level of support. Current prices are always on [dapta.ai/pricing](https://dapta.ai/pricing) and in the **Plans** tab of **Billing & Plans**.

## Which Dapta plan should I choose and what does it cost?

**Pro** is US$99 a month with 100,000 credits, 5 users and 5 workspaces. **Scale-Up** is US$499 with 500,000 credits, unlimited users and 25 workspaces. **Prime** is US$1,499 with 1,500,000 credits, 70 workspaces and a dedicated account manager. **Enterprise** is priced by Sales. Pay yearly and you save 16.7%, about two months free.

## Monthly or yearly

The **Plans** tab has a **Monthly** / **Yearly** switch. Yearly billing is 16.7% cheaper than paying month by month, the equivalent of two months free, and is charged once a year. Your monthly credit allowance is the same on both.

***

## Free trial

New accounts can start **Pro** with a **7-day free trial**. You add a card when you start, and the trial gives you the plan's 100,000 credits so you can run real calls and messages from day one.

* If you do nothing, the card is charged the Pro price at the end of day 7 and your subscription continues. The 100,000 credits from the trial are your first month's allowance; the payment does not add another 100,000.
* If Dapta is not for you, cancel from **Billing & Plans → Plans → Cancel Plan** before the trial ends and nothing is charged.
* If you already know you want to stay, click **Pay now** on your plan card in the **Billing** tab. It ends the trial immediately and charges the first month.

Scale-Up and Prime do not include a trial. If you have not upgraded yet and want to try a bigger plan, talk to Sales from the **Plans** tab.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The trial charge is automatic. If you do not want to be billed, cancel before day 7 ends. See [Cancel your subscription](/my-account/how-credits-work-in-dapta/cancel-your-subscription.md).
{% endhint %}

## Without a paid plan

A free account lets you explore the platform and build agents, but the monthly credit allowance, free web calls and Test Numbers need an active paid plan. On a free account a web call from the browser costs 200 credits per minute, and Dapti is charged per answer.

***

## Estimate what you need

Not sure which plan covers your volume? Two quick ways to find out:

1. Click **Calculate your potential usage** at the top of the **Plans** tab and enter your expected calls and messages.
2. Ask Dapti: *"How many credits would it cost to run 500 calls a month of 2 minutes each?"* Dapti uses the same rates as Billing and tells you which plan covers it.

As a reference, an answered call costs 333 credits per minute, so Pro covers about 300 minutes of conversation a month, Scale-Up about 1,500 and Prime about 4,500, before messages, numbers and Dapti.

***

## Enterprise and custom plans

The **Enterprise** card in the **Plans** tab is for companies that need more than Prime: consumption-based credits, a custom number of workspaces, premium support with service-level agreements, enterprise-grade security and on-premise deployment. Click **Talk to Sales** on the card, or book a demo from [dapta.ai](https://www.dapta.ai/). Enterprise agreements can be invoiced and paid by bank transfer instead of card.
