 Billing and credits 
 What a Dapta credit is worth and what each call, message, phone number and Dapti answer costs. The same rates Support quotes, in one table. 
 Credits are the single currency for everything you do in Dapta: AI calls, text messages, phone numbers, Dapti and Flow Studio. This page is the reference for what each one costs. 
 How much does a call, a message or a phone number cost? 
 [->#how-much-does-a-call-a-message-or-a-phone-number-cost] 
 1,000 credits = US$1. An answered call costs 333 credits per minute , rounded up to the whole minute. A call that reaches voicemail costs 75 , an AI text message 40 , and a US phone number about 1,380 credits per month . Calls to your registered Test Number are free. Every rate is in the table below.

 Voice calls 
 [->#voice-calls] 
 To generate an AI call, Dapta pays for a language model (such as GPT or Claude), speech-to-text and text-to-speech (such as ElevenLabs or Deepgram) and a telephony carrier. All of that is consolidated into one rate per minute. 
 What each call outcome costs 
 [->#what-each-call-outcome-costs] 
 Answered call: 333 credits per minute. Billing is per minute, not per second, and every started minute counts as a whole minute: 
 A call of 1 minute 58 seconds bills 2 minutes = 666 credits 
 A call of 1 minute 20 seconds bills 2 minutes = 666 credits 
 A call of 45 seconds bills 1 minute = 333 credits 
 Voicemail: 75 credits per call. The call is still placed and the AI has to detect the voicemail. A call counts as voicemail only when the carrier answers with a standard tone or a non-personalized message. If the recipient recorded their own greeting, the call is treated as answered and bills a full minute. Enable the End call option on your agent to hang up before a second minute starts. 
 Not answered or busy: 45 credits per call. 
 Failed calls: 0 credits. Invalid numbers, declined calls and carrier errors are not charged. 
 Test calls 
 [->#test-calls] 
 Register your own phone as a Test Number and every call to it is free. It appears in the Call Log labeled Test Call with its cost shown but not deducted from your balance. A call to any other number consumes credits, even when you are testing. Test Numbers are available on paid plans, including the trial; you can add a new one once a month, and you still need a positive balance to place the call. 
 [->/ai-voice-agents/phone-numbers/how-to-add-a-phone-number-for-testing-in-dapta] How to add a Phone Number for Testing in Dapta 
 Web calls 
 [->#web-calls] 
 A web call is a call from your browser to the agent. It is free on every paid plan. On the Free plan it costs 200 credits per minute. 
 Phone numbers 
 [->#phone-numbers] 
 US numbers you buy in Dapta 
 [->#us-numbers-you-buy-in-dapta] 
 Go to Voice Agents → Phone Numbers → Buy Phone Number . Before you confirm, Dapta shows the monthly price in credits: the carrier's monthly price multiplied by 1.2, which is about 1,380 credits per month for a standard US local number. 
 The first month is prorated: you pay only for the days left in your billing period. 
 From then on, the full monthly price is deducted from your balance on the number's billing date. 
 The calls you make and receive on the number are charged on top, at the call rates above. 
 [->/ai-voice-agents/phone-numbers/buying-phone-numbers-through-dapta] Buying Phone Numbers through Dapta

 Text agents and WhatsApp 
 [->#text-agents-and-whatsapp] 
 Every AI answer costs 40 credits , whichever model the agent uses. One answer means the AI reads an incoming message and replies once, however long the reply is. A message a person sends from the Inbox costs 10 credits, and the messages your contacts send you are free. 
 Choose the model on how well it answers, not on price. The agent's settings show which models can handle images and files. The AI can process voice notes but replies in text. 
 WhatsApp message delivery 
 [->#whatsapp-message-delivery] 
 On WhatsApp, Meta charges for delivering the messages , templates and replies alike. It charges in money, to your own Meta account. Those charges do not consume Dapta credits and do not appear on your Dapta invoice. Dapta charges for the agent, Meta charges for delivery, and nothing is charged twice. 
 For Meta's rates, the free allowances and how to set up the payment method, see [->/ai-text-agents/channels/whatsapp-billing-and-meta-charges] WhatsApp billing 
 . 
 Was your line connected before Dapta moved to connecting directly with Meta? Its messaging is still billed in credits. See [->/ai-text-agents/channels/legacy-line-billing] Billing on legacy lines 
 . 
 Dapti 
 [->#dapti] 
 Dapti is Dapta's co-pilot that helps you build voice and text agents using natural language. It is charged per answer , not per minute and not by the number of tools it uses internally: each answer costs the real model cost multiplied by 2, so the price depends on how complex the interaction was.

 An average Dapti answer costs about 150 credits ($0.15). 
 Credits are deducted automatically as each answer is charged. 
 Free and Pro plans pay for each Dapti answer. On Scale-Up, Prime and Enterprise Dapti is included at no additional cost. 
 There is no message limit, only your balance: while you have credits you can use Dapti without restrictions. 
 Flow Studio automations do not consume Dapti credits; they are charged separately as described below. 
 Flow Studio 
 [->#flow-studio] 
 A flow run costs 1 credit , regardless of how many records it processes. 
 An AI node that uses Dapta's connection adds the model's real cost multiplied by 2. The node shows this on its price meter before you run it. 
 An AI node that uses your own credential does not use Dapta credits; the model vendor bills your own account. 
 Plans and what happens when credits run out 
 [->#plans-and-what-happens-when-credits-run-out] 
 Every plan includes a monthly credit allowance: Pro 100,000, Scale Up 500,000, Prime 1,500,000, and consumption-based credits on Custom plans. Current plans and prices are always at [->https://dapta.ai/pricing] dapta.ai/pricing 
 , and your balance is under Billing in the admin panel. 
 When your balance reaches 0 , calls, messages and Dapti stop until credits are added. There are no overage charges. 
 Credits belong to an active subscription. If your plan is cancelled or expires, the remaining credits are not carried over. 
 You can buy extra credits at any time under Billing , in multiples of 1,000 credits ($1). Credits you buy do not expire while your subscription is active; they accumulate on top of your monthly allowance. 
 With auto-recharge on, credits are added automatically when your balance drops to 20,000 ($20). 
 When someone you refer makes their first payment, they receive bonus credits and you receive $50 . The details are on the referral page.
