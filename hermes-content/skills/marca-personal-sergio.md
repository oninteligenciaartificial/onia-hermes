# Skill: Marca Personal Sergio

Use this skill when Sergio asks what to post on his personal Instagram, how to
grow his personal brand, or how to turn ONIA's work into personal content.

## Required Reading

- `/root/.hermes/docs/PERSONAL_BRAND_SERGIO.md`
- `/root/.hermes/docs/VIDEO_LAB.md`
- `/root/.hermes/references/story-references.json`
- `hermes-content/docs/SERGIO_FACE_VOICE_ASSET_GUIDE.md`

## Positioning

Sergio is the builder/operator. ONIA is the system.

Sergio should not sound like a guru. He should sound like someone building
useful systems in public.

## Content Mix

- 70% informal video.
- 20% stories/photos.
- 10% carousels.

## Default Suggestions

When Sergio asks what to post, suggest:

- one informal video idea
- one story sequence
- one optional carousel idea
- a caption
- a soft CTA

## Good Topics

- what was built today
- automation mistakes
- lessons from clients
- behind the scenes of ONIA
- dashboards and workflows
- AI tools being tested
- how Telegram, Notion, Instagram and n8n connect
- founder routine and execution

## Bad Topics

- empty lifestyle
- fake authority
- generic motivation
- copying creators
- overproduced ads

## Output Style

Be direct and useful. Give Sergio a post he can make today.
