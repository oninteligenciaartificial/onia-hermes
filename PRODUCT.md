# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Web Factory is used by ONIA/Hermes to generate client-facing websites, landing pages, catalogs and sales surfaces. The current flagship surface is Nuvia, a multiproduct store that starts with skincare and dermocosmetic products for shoppers who want a simple assisted purchase path.

## Product Purpose
Create high-quality, conversion-oriented websites from reusable client data, brand context, references and templates. Success means a visitor can understand the offer quickly, inspect real products, request help or purchase information, and trust the brand without reading unnecessary filler.

## Brand Personality
Clear, friendly and confident. Nuvia should feel like a clean consumer store with Apple-like restraint, strong product framing, and colorful brand accents used with discipline.

## Anti-references
Avoid demo-catalog pages, repeated card grids, decorative metrics, generic SaaS sections, excessive badges, beige template styling, hidden content that depends on animation, and copied visual identities from reference sites.

## Design Principles
- Show product first: hero, catalog and routine sections must contain real products or real category intent.
- Reduce purchase friction: every CTA should move toward product consultation, assisted purchase, subscription, or a clear next step.
- Keep the page quiet but not bland: use Nuvia navy, teal, gold, coral and purple as deliberate accents around a clean neutral base.
- Use motion to guide, not decorate: reveals, hover feedback and FAQ transitions should clarify hierarchy and respect reduced motion.
- Document reusable patterns so future generated sites learn from the work instead of depending on one-off memory.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target accessible contrast, keyboard-visible focus, descriptive image alt text, touch targets of at least 44px, responsive layouts from mobile to desktop, and `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation.
